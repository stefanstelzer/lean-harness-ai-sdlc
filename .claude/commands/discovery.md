You are running **discovery** for a new feature (the entry station of the
Feature flow in WORKFLOW.md). Do not jump to a solution. Instead:

1. **Problem** — restate the underlying user/business problem.
2. **Users & jobs** — who is affected and what job are they trying to do.
3. **Existing landscape** — search the codebase for related code, prior art and
   constraints. Cite files as `path:line`.
4. **Risks & unknowns** — technical, product and delivery risks.
5. **Hypotheses** — 2–3 candidate directions with rough trade-offs.
6. **Recommended next step** — usually `/grill-me-with-context` then `/prd-to-plan`.

Prefer reading the repo over assuming. Surface what is _not_ yet known.
